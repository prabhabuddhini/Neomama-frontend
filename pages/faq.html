NeoMama is a digital maternal and newborn healthcare platform designed to help doctors and clinics manage patient records, monitor pregnancy progress, and track baby health efficiently.

NeoMama is mainly designed for doctor and midwife who manage maternal and newborn healthcare records.

The web platform is designed for doctor and midwife. The NeoMama mobile application is intended for mothers and parents to track pregnancy progress, reminders, and baby health information.

NeoMama includes both a web platform for doctor and midwife and a mobile application concept for mothers and parents.

NeoMama supports patient record management, pregnancy monitoring, baby record management, baby growth tracking, reports and analytics, alerts, and clinic workflow support.

Doctors can log in through the NeoMama web platform login page using their account credentials.
