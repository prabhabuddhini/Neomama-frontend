Privacy Policy

Learn how NeoMama collects, uses, stores, and protects healthcare-related information.

Introduction

NeoMama respects user privacy and is committed to protecting healthcare-related information. The platform is designed to support secure maternal and newborn healthcare management for doctors and midwives.

NeoMama is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of maternal and newborn healthcare data. The platform collects only the information necessary to manage clinic operations, patient records, and health monitoring features.

Information We Collect

  • User account details
  • Doctor / midwife information
  • Patient basic details
  • Pregnancy monitoring data
  • Baby growth and health records
  • Clinic visit records
  • System usage data

How We Use Information

  • Manage maternal records
  • Monitor pregnancy progress
  • Track newborn health
  • Generate reports
  • Improve clinic workflow
  • Maintain security and system performance

Data Protection

NeoMama uses secure storage methods, restricted access controls, and authorized-user-only visibility to help protect healthcare-related information against unauthorized access.

Data Sharing

NeoMama does not sell personal data. Information is only used within the clinic and system context and may be shared only when required for healthcare operations or legal obligations.

User Responsibilities

Users should keep login credentials safe, avoid sharing passwords, and use the system responsibly to maintain account and patient data security.

NeoMama is designed to store patient records securely and allow access only to authorized users such as doctors and approved midwives. The system aims to maintain a safe digital healthcare environment without selling or misusing personal data.
