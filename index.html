How It Works

NeoMama connects doctor, midwife, mothers, and parents through a digital platform that supports maternal and newborn healthcare management.

For Doctors & Clinic Staff

1️⃣
Log Into the Web Platform

Doctor and Midwife access the web system through the login page.

2️⃣
Register or View Patient Records

Users can register mothers and manage patient profiles digitally.

3️⃣
Monitor Pregnancy Health

Track blood pressure, weight, symptoms, and pregnancy progress.

4️⃣
Record Baby Information

Add baby details such as birth date, weight, height, and notes.

5️⃣
Track Baby Growth Data

Monitor growth indicators with charts and WHO-based comparisons.

6️⃣
View Reports and Alerts

Review analytics, clinic performance, and AI-based health alerts.

For Mothers & Parents

1️⃣
Create an Account

Mothers and parents can securely register in the mobile application.

2️⃣
Track Pregnancy Progress

Record pregnancy health details and monitor weekly progress.

3️⃣
Receive Health Tips and Reminders

Get nutrition guidance, clinic reminders, and health tips.

4️⃣
Store Baby Records Digitally

Maintain baby growth details and clinic-related information digitally.

5️⃣
Monitor Vaccinations and Clinic Visits

Stay updated with vaccination schedules and regular clinic checkups.

6️⃣
Use Smart Support Features

Access chatbot support and baby symptom guidance when needed.
